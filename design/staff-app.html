Staff App

Serif carries anything a person reads at arm's length; mono carries anything they scan for a number. Sans does the work in between. No sans or serif text below 15px, and nothing tappable below 19px; mono labels run 10.5–13px, caps only, at 55–60% cream.

Rules for a dim room
  • Largest lit area on any screen stays under 20% — no white sheets, no filled cards.
  • Brass is the only fill. One filled button per screen, maximum.
  • Dim past states (passed bookings, ticked items) to ~45–62% rather than hiding them.
  • Elevation by hairline and a half-step darker surface, never by shadow or gradient.
  • Locks itself after 2 minutes on the iPad; a phone follows the OS.
  • No red badges, no counts that climb, no push. Nudges are stated once and stay put.
