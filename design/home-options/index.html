Changed in all three

  • Soul Night appears once. Today it has its own full-width band and then comes back as the first What's On row. The options fold it into What's On and make it the lead item.
  • Booking asks only what gets sent. The live widget passes only party size to Cal; the dates and times drawn on the page are decoration. The options ask for party size, then "Choose a time" opens Cal's real availability.
  • No doodles, pattern fills, floating cards or dividers. Each option keeps at most the crescent mark.
  • Fewer type voices. Two typefaces each instead of three.

Still to decide

  • Photography. A and C have labelled photo slots. Are there photos of the bar, the Lounge and the boards to use?
  • Light or dark. B is dark all the way through; A and C are light with one dark block for booking.
  • The illustrations. Any option can bring one drawing back (for example the moon from the opener) if losing them all feels too plain.
  • Mixing. Parts combine well, for example C's contents index with A's photography.
